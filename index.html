Or draw one

Turn on painting and drag across the grid. Pick a brush: step a square one shade darker or lighter, or paint a shade straight on. Anything you change keeps a gold outline until it goes back to where it started.

Brush

Daydream is a five-note scale with soft piano and strings. More styles arrive through the year, each with its own instruments and harmony. Rows become pitch — Sunday is the highest voice, Saturday the lowest. Shade becomes loudness, and the deepest green adds a bell above the note. A busy week fills a whole bar; a week with one or two commits takes half of one; an empty week passes in a single beat. Underneath it all, a bass and a string pad keep an eight-bar progression turning without pause, so a gap in the year drifts rather than stops — and when nothing is happening up top, the pad picks out a quiet figure of its own.

Keep the recording

Records one full pass as an audio file while it plays, so the file sounds exactly like what you hear. Takes as long as the piece does.

Looping is ignored during a recording, and changing the grid mid-record will restart nothing — stop and start again instead.

Nothing leaves your browser. Every note is generated live from the grid.

Credits, notices and the boring bits

The music is original

No part of what Contrib Suite plays is a sample, a recording, or a transcription of an existing piece. Every note is synthesised in your browser at the moment it sounds, from three ingredients: a five-note scale, an eight-bar chord progression, and whatever is on the grid. Two different years produce two different pieces, and a grid you draw yourself produces something nobody has heard before. The sound is written in the general idiom of pentatonic film scoring — an idiom, not a particular composer’s work.

Your data stays with you

The grid, your edits and the handle you last typed are kept in your own browser’s local storage and are sent nowhere. There is no account, no analytics and no server behind this page. When you use the live fetch, your request goes directly from your browser to the contributions API named below; nothing passes through us. Clearing your browser’s site data for this page removes everything it has kept.

A contribution graph is public, but it is also a fairly intimate picture of how somebody spends their days. Please point this at your own profile, or at one whose owner would not mind.

About that console line

The by-hand route asks you to paste one line of JavaScript into your browser’s developer console. That is, in general, exactly how people get scammed, so here is precisely what this one does and nothing else: it reads the date and the shade of every square already drawn on the page you are standing on, turns them into a list, and puts that list on your clipboard. It sends nothing, changes nothing and signs you into nothing. The line is short enough to read in full before you run it, and you should. Never run a longer one you have not read.
